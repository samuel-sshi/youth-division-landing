✦ What we believe

Church built for the next generation.

We're ONEIGHTY — a 180 from where you were. Come as you are: no filters, no pretense. Here you'll find a community that knows you, faith that grows you, and a chance to step up as a leader. And when you're ready, we go — to school, to the neighborhood, to everyone God puts in your path.
